Take 50 pages from a meeting, merge them into a single PDF, add a password, and share it through any app on your phone. Print directly, send via email, or drop it into Drive. The export pipeline handles large batches and produces clean, compact files.

## Common use cases

- Email a 30-page contract to a client in a single attachment
- Send a password-protected PDF of sensitive documents
- Print a multi-page report straight from your phone
- Export an entire folder as one PDF for archival
