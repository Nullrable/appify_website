Pro is a single subscription that unlocks three improvements designed for heavy users. The free tier is fully usable for most people; Pro removes the limits and the ads. Subscriptions are managed by your app store — cancel anytime, no questions asked.

## Common use cases

- Scan hundreds of pages a week without hitting a daily OCR cap
- Use the app for work without the documents banner above your library
- Forget about manually pushing scans to the cloud — it just happens
- Support continued development of the app
