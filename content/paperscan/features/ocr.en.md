OCR runs entirely on-device using Apple's and Google's machine learning kits. Select text inside a scan, copy it to another app, or let the search bar index every page in your library. The free tier includes a generous daily allowance; Pro removes the cap for users who scan hundreds of pages a week.

## Common use cases

- Copy a phone number or address off a scanned business card
- Search a year's worth of receipts for one vendor
- Quote a passage from a scanned book without retyping
- Bulk-OCR an inbox of paper to make it searchable

## Pro

> Pro removes the daily OCR cap and processes your entire library in one go.
