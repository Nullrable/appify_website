Most phone cameras force you to stand up and walk backwards to fit a large page. The ultra-wide lens keeps the whole document in frame while you stay seated. Edge detection, perspective correction, and shadow removal all happen the moment you finish the shot, so you see a clean, flat page instantly.

## Common use cases

- Snap a full A3 handout on a meeting-room table without standing up
- Capture an entire whiteboard in a single shot instead of stitching
- Scan restaurant menus, instruction booklets, or magazine spreads
- Take a multi-page document apart from the bound edition — page by page
