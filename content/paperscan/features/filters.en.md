Filters run on the device GPU, so the preview is instant. Choose the look that fits the document: clean black & white for printed text, magic color for faded receipts, photo mode for preserves, and more. Adjust brightness, contrast, and shadow after the fact — every change stays on-device.

## Common use cases

- Black & white for contracts and printed forms
- Magic color for restoring faded receipts and old book pages
- Photo mode when you want to keep the original look
- Quick brightness/contrast tweak when lighting was uneven
