## Stitch Images

Turn a set of images into a single long image or collage without a complex editor. Pick images, choose a stitch mode, export — done in three steps.

## Three Stitch Modes

- **Vertical**: Images stack top to bottom. Good for stitching chat-log screenshots or long article screenshots into a single tall image.
- **Horizontal**: Images line up left to right. Good for side-by-side comparisons, like before-and-after screenshots.
- **Grid**: Images arrange into a grid. Good for grouping a set of similarly styled images, like multiple shots of the same scene.

## Original Aspect Ratio Preserved

Stitching doesn't stretch or crop images. Each image keeps its original aspect ratio, packed together tightly. The exported image has the same clarity as the originals — no quality loss from stitching.

## Preview and Adjust

Preview the result before stitching, and drag images to reorder them. Once it looks right, export. The output goes through the system share sheet and is saved to local history. The whole processing is local — images are not uploaded to any server.
