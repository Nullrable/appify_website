## PDF Merge

Combine multiple PDFs into one complete file. Scans, contract attachments, multi-chapter documents, or files saved at different times that you finally need in one bundle — you can do it all on your phone.

## Multi-Select with Thumbnail Preview

Pick multiple PDFs at once. The App generates a thumbnail for each one so you can visually confirm what's in every file and avoid picking the wrong one or missing one.

## Drag to Set Order

Order matters — contract body first, attachments after, or arrange by time. Drag the thumbnails to set where each PDF goes in the merged result, then merge once you're happy.

## Local Processing, Auto-Recorded

Merging runs locally on your device, so files never get uploaded to any server. The merged result goes through the system share sheet and is saved to local history. You can find and re-share it any time from history.
