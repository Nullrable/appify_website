## Image to PDF

Turn photos on your phone into a single PDF in three steps: pick, order, export. Everything runs locally on your device, so your images never get uploaded to any server.

## Two Image Sources

Once you're in the Image to PDF feature, you can pick images in bulk from two sources:

- **Photos**: Multi-select from your album. Good for content shot on your phone.
- **Files app**: Pick from iCloud Drive or a local folder. Good for images already archived.

Supported formats include JPG, PNG, HEIC/HEIF, WebP, BMP, GIF — iPhone's default HEIC photos work directly, no need to convert formats first.

## Drag to Reorder

Selected images appear as a grid. Long-press and drag to change the page order in the PDF. For materials where page order matters — contracts, receipts, IDs — check each page before exporting.

## Export at Original Size

Each PDF page takes its size from the original image. No scaling, no margins added, so the image's clarity is fully preserved in the PDF. After export, the file goes through the system share sheet, and the result is also saved to local history for easy re-sharing later.
