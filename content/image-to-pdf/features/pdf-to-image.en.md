## PDF to Image

Turn one or more pages of a PDF into images — useful for inserting into documents, sending to someone without a PDF reader, or saving a single page as image content. Preview every page, pick by page, and only export what you need.

## Page-by-Page Preview and Selection

Once you pick the PDF, the App renders a thumbnail preview of every page. You can browse through them and check the pages you want to export. Select All and Clear are both one tap away. Only need page 3 and page 7? Check those two and export just them — no need to convert the whole PDF into images.

## Five Scale Levels for Clarity

Before exporting, pick a render scale factor: 1.0x, 1.5x, 2.0x, 2.5x, 3.0x.

- Higher scale means higher PNG resolution, sharper detail, larger files.
- For on-screen viewing, 1.0x or 1.5x is usually enough. For printing or zoom-in detail work, 2.0x or above is recommended.

## Export and Share

Once you've confirmed your selection, the App exports the checked pages as PNG images. They go through the system share sheet, and the conversion is saved to local history. The whole render is local — the PDF is not uploaded to any server.
