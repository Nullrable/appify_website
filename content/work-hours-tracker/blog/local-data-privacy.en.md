## Where Should Work-Hours Data Live? Local Storage and Privacy

When choosing a time-tracking tool, most people only look at features and rarely ask one question: where do these records end up, and who can see them?

## Timesheet Records Are More Sensitive Than You Think

A complete timesheet reveals far more than "what time you clock in and out": it shows your work patterns, rest habits, and income level — and with multiple jobs, your entire allocation of time. If that information leaks or gets misused, the risks are real.

## Cloud Account vs Local Storage

Tools that require an account and sync data to servers mean your records exist, at least partly, somewhere outside your control: a server breach, a change in the company's privacy policy, or the service shutting down can all affect your data. With a local-storage tool, the data lives only on your device, and everything works fully offline.

## How Work Hours Tracker Does It

Work Hours Tracker takes a thoroughly local approach:

- **All data is stored locally on the device** — no account, nothing uploaded to servers;
- **Fully offline-capable**: clock in, view records, and check statistics with no network at all;
- **Biometric app lock**: enable fingerprint or Face ID unlock so others cannot open the app and browse your records;
- **Backups stay in your hands**: backup files are saved by you through sharing — you control exactly where they go.

For anyone who takes privacy seriously, this "data never leaves the device" design is far more reassuring to use.
