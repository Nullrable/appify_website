## How Is Night-Shift Pay Calculated? Tracking and Verification

Pay accounting for shift work is often more complicated than nine-to-five: is there a night allowance, which day does a shift crossing midnight belong to, how do night hours stack with overtime — questions that make many people give up checking their own pay. But with the right records, verification is not hard.

## Common Components of Night-Shift Pay

Night income usually consists of:

- **Base hourly rate**: the same as day shifts;
- **Night allowance or multiplier**: some industries and regions pay a supplement or premium for late-night hours (for example, 22:00 to 06:00);
- **Stacked overtime**: if the total night shift exceeds the overtime threshold, the excess is also paid at overtime multipliers.

The specifics vary by country, region, industry, and contract — your employment contract and local rules are the deciding references.

## Records Come First

Manual night-shift records go wrong easily: shifts crossing midnight, hours extended on short notice, schedules changed mid-month — reconstructing them from memory is nearly impossible. That is also why many night workers lose out without realizing it.
