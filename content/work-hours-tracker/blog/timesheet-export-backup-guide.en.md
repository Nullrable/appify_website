## A Practical Guide to Exporting and Backing Up Timesheets

Three months of records, then a lost or replaced phone — everything gone. That is the shared risk of everyone who keeps their timesheet only on their phone. Export and backup solve the problem, with plenty of immediate benefits too.

## Why Export

- **Pay verification**: sending the timesheet to your employer, or matching it against your payslip, beats an oral check;
- **Evidence**: in a labor dispute, day-by-day clock records are strong supporting material;
- **Data analysis**: exported data can be analyzed further in spreadsheet software;
- **Device migration**: restore from a backup in one step instead of starting over.

## Choosing Among Three Formats

- **CSV**: the most universal, for importing into Excel, Numbers, and other tools;
- **PDF**: fixed layout, for printing or showing to someone directly;
- **Excel**: the most complete, with an overtime breakdown worksheet covering tiered overtime and night-shift items — ideal for careful checks.

Files can be shared directly after exporting. Exporting is a Pro feature.

## The Right Way to Back Up

Back up all data regularly, and save the file to cloud storage or a computer via sharing — the copy on the phone plus one elsewhere is what counts as safe. Restoring simply selects the backup file. You keep the backup files yourself; nothing is uploaded to any server.
