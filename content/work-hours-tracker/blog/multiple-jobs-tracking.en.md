## How to Track Hours for Multiple Jobs Separately

Plenty of people work several jobs at once: full-time during the day, part-time at night, odd jobs on weekends. The extra money is nice, but the bookkeeping doubles — different hourly rates, different schedules, even different overtime rules per job. Mix them together and month-end becomes one big muddle.

## Three Keys to Separate Tracking

- **Record per job**: only separate records per job show each one's true earnings;
- **Separate rates and rules**: different jobs have different rates, and the applicable overtime rules may differ too;
- **Review regularly**: the biggest risk of multiple jobs is unbalanced time investment — one job eats your hours while paying poorly.

## Managing Multiple Jobs with the App

Work Hours Tracker supports adding multiple jobs. When clocking in, choose the job; each job's records and hourly rate stay independent. The job distribution chart in the statistics shows each job's share of hours and income — which job takes the most time and which pays best per hour is visible at a glance.

For anyone looking to reallocate time toward better-paying work, that share chart beats gut feeling every time.
