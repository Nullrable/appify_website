## Automatic Pay and Overtime Calculation

Enter your hourly rate in settings and the app calculates earnings for every record automatically. Regular hours are paid at your base rate, overtime hours are multiplied according to your regional overtime rules — no calculator needed.

## Tiered Overtime Multipliers

In many regions overtime rates step up progressively: 1.5x after the daily threshold, then 2x beyond a longer duration. Both the built-in presets and custom rules support these tiers, with hours and pay accumulated separately per tier.

## Night Shifts and Weekly Accumulation

If your rules involve night work, you can record night-shift periods and the app tracks night-shift hours and pay separately. For regions that accumulate overtime weekly, the app also totals weekly hours and counts the excess as overtime.

## Multiple Currencies

The currency used to display earnings can be freely selected to match how you are actually paid. Switching currencies only affects display — none of your records are modified.
