## Statistics Charts: See Where Your Time Goes

A single record only reflects one day. To answer questions like "how many hours did I work this month", "is my income growing", or "which job takes the most time", you need statistics charts. Work Hours Tracker provides three types:

## Work-Hours Trend

Shows how your hours change over time. Busy and slow periods are visible at a glance — ideal for reviewing how intense a period of work was.

## Earning Trend

Shows how income changes over time. Compared with the hours trend, you can tell whether income growth comes from more hours or a better rate.

## Job Distribution

If you have multiple jobs, the distribution chart shows each job's share of hours and income, helping you put time into what pays better.

All charts are generated from local records in real time and update automatically with every clock-in — no manual maintenance.
