## 13 Labor Law Presets

Overtime rules differ widely between countries and regions: the daily threshold where overtime starts, the multipliers, and the rules for rest days and public holidays all vary. Work Hours Tracker ships with presets for these 13 countries/regions:

- China
- United States (federal)
- California, US
- Japan
- South Korea
- Germany
- France
- United Kingdom
- Australia
- Brazil
- India
- Mexico
- Singapore

After selecting a preset, the app automatically calculates overtime hours and pay according to the common rules of that region.

## Fully Custom Overtime Rules

If your contract differs from the preset, or your country/region is not on the list, switch to custom rules: the daily overtime threshold and the multiplier for each tier can all be set yourself. Once configured, every record and statistic is recalculated under your rules.

## Rules and Records Work Together

The overtime rule applies to every clock-in record. After switching rules or adjusting parameters, hours and earnings statistics are presented under the current rules, so you can verify everything against your actual situation.
