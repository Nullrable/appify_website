## Public Holiday Syncing

Holiday arrangements directly affect work planning and overtime accounting: how to schedule around makeup workdays, and whether working on a holiday counts as rest-day overtime — it all depends on whether the day is an official holiday. Work Hours Tracker can automatically sync public holidays for these 15 countries/regions:

China, Hong Kong (China), Taiwan (China), Japan, South Korea, Singapore, United States, California (US), United Kingdom, Germany, France, Australia, Brazil, India, Mexico.

## Clear in Your Calendar

Once synced, holidays are clearly marked in the calendar view. No more browsing websites for holiday schedules when planning shifts — open the app to see which days off are coming this month.

## Service and Privacy Notes

Holiday data is fetched from a public holiday API (Nager.Date) and only connects to the network during syncing; results are stored locally. Your work-hour records themselves are never uploaded to any server.
