## One-Tap Clock In and Out

Timing starts with a single tap. Tap the button when you start work and the app begins timing immediately; tap again when you finish and the record is saved automatically. You never have to type in any times by hand.

## Today's Hours and Earnings at a Glance

The clock page shows the live timer and adds up today's hours and estimated earnings. No more mental math about how long you actually worked today, and no digging through chat logs for clock-in screenshots — just open the app.

## Automatic Reminder When You Enter Overtime

When the accumulated hours of your current shift cross into the overtime range, the app sends a local notification. Overtime is counted separately from that moment, making it easy to verify your overtime pay — and reminding you it might be time to head home.

## From Tap to Record, Fully Automatic

Every clock-in is archived automatically with the start time, end time, duration, and the results calculated under your overtime rules. Afterwards you can review them on the records page, and see longer-term patterns in the statistics charts.
