This Privacy Policy explains how **Work Hours Tracker** (the "**App**") handles information about you. In short: your work-hours data stays on your device — we don't sell it, don't collect it, and can't see it.

We value your privacy and built the App with a "local-first" design to honor that commitment.

## 1. We Collect No Personal Data

The App requires no account registration and asks for no name, email, or phone number. All your data — clock records, hourly rates, overtime rules — is stored in the app's local database on your device and **is never uploaded to our servers or any third party's**.

We don't sell your data; we have no data to sell.

## 2. Data Stored Locally

The following data exists only on your device:

- Clock-in and work-hours records
- Job information and hourly-rate settings
- Overtime rules and preset selection
- App settings (currency, start of week, etc.)

Because this data exists only on your device, we cannot access, view, or recover it. You can view, modify, or delete it in the app at any time, or wipe everything whenever you like.

## 3. Biometric App Lock

If you enable the app-lock feature, fingerprint or face verification is performed by your device's system biometric interface (such as Touch ID / Face ID). The App **does not store or capture your fingerprint or face data** — the verification is handled entirely by the system.

## 4. Network Usage

The App's core features (clocking, calculations, statistics, export) **work fully offline**. The only feature that involves the network is statutory-holiday syncing:

- When syncing holidays, the app queries a public holiday data API (**Nager.Date**) for the region you select
- The request **contains none** of your personal data or work records — only the selected country/region code
- If you don't need this feature, simply skip holiday syncing; the rest of the app is unaffected

## 5. Pro Subscription

Payment for the Pro subscription is handled by the **Apple App Store**, and subscription verification and entitlement management run through **RevenueCat**. We never see your payment details (card number, billing address, etc.) and keep no payment credentials. The relevant transaction data is governed by the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and the [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://revenuecat.com/privacy/).

## 6. Backups and Sharing

Backup and export files you create through the app are controlled entirely by you: where the files go through the system share sheet (cloud storage, email, a computer) is entirely up to you. We cannot obtain these files and receive no notifications about them.

## 7. Data Deletion

Because everything is local, deletion is thorough:

- You can delete individual records or clear all data in the app
- Uninstalling the app removes all local data from the device

These actions are irreversible — back up first.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the minimum contract age of applicable law, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after an update takes effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. If there are material changes, we will provide reasonable notice in the App or on the App Store page.
