## Freeform Crop

A screenshot with extra borders, a subject that's too small in the frame, an image that has to match a platform's specific aspect ratio — all of these call for cropping. Image Converter uses the system-level crop tool, with an intuitive interface and full functionality.

## Common Cropping Scenarios

- **Remove watermarks or borders**: Screenshots and downloaded images often come with extra borders or white margins. Crop them away.
- **Emphasize the subject**: The subject is too small in the photo. Crop out the surroundings to make it stand out.
- **Match a platform's ratio**: Crop to 1:1 (avatars), 4:3 (horizontal banners), 16:9 (video covers), and other standard ratios.
- **Adapt to social media**: Turn a landscape photo into 9:16 for Stories, or a portrait photo into 1.91:1 for Weibo / X header images.

## Fixed Ratios and Freeform Cropping

The app supports two cropping modes:

- **Fixed ratio**: Choose a common ratio such as 1:1, 4:3, 16:9, or 9:16. The crop frame locks to that ratio for fast, standard-sized output.
- **Freeform**: Drag any of the four corners of the crop frame freely. Ideal for irregular trimming.

## Cropping Stays on Your Device

Cropping runs locally on your device. Neither the original image nor the cropped result is uploaded to a server. For full details on how your data is handled, see the [Privacy Policy](/en/image-converter/privacy/).
