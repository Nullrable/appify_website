## Image Stitching

A chat log you want to share as one long screenshot, side-by-side product comparisons, a stack of themed photos turned into a contact sheet — these situations all need multiple images combined into one. Image Converter offers three stitching modes that cover the most common use cases.

## Three Stitching Modes

- **Vertical stitching**: Images are stacked top to bottom. Ideal for chat logs, long article screenshots, and tutorial step images, producing one complete long image.
- **Horizontal stitching**: Images are placed left to right. Great for side-by-side comparisons such as different product options or different angles of the same scene.
- **3×3 grid**: Images are arranged in a grid. Perfect for organizing a set of stylistically consistent photos, a nine-image social media post, or a contact sheet.

## Up to 20 Images

The app supports combining up to 20 images in a single stitch (up to 9 in grid mode). Exceeding the limit triggers a prompt, preventing oversized output files.

## Reorder Freely

Once you've picked your images, the app displays them all in a grid where you can:

- **Drag to reorder**: Long-press an image and drag it to its new position.
- **Delete an image**: Tap the delete button at the top-right corner of an image.
- **Add more**: Tap "Add more" to keep selecting images.

Switch between stitching modes after reordering to preview the result. What you see is what you get.

## Stitching Stays on Your Device

The entire stitching process runs locally on your device. Neither the originals nor the stitched result are uploaded to a server. For full details on how your data is handled, see the [Privacy Policy](/en/image-converter/privacy/).
