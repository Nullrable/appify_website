## Why So Many Levels

Image compression is a lossy operation — the more detail you drop, the smaller the file and the worse the quality. The real question is "how much loss is acceptable?" — sharing on social media and printing photos demand very different standards. Image Converter provides nine presets from 10 to 90, plus a free slider from 1 to 99, covering everything from extreme compression to archival quality.

## How the Nine Presets Actually Perform

### 10–30: Extreme Compression

- **File size**: 5–15% of the original.
- **Quality**: Clearly visible loss to the naked eye. Best for pure-text screenshots and flat-color images.
- **Typical scenarios**: WeChat stickers, long text-only screenshots, temporary sharing.

For example, a 5 MB photo compressed at level 10 may end up only 200–400 KB. But facial features and landscape detail will blur away.

### 40–60: Everyday Sharing

- **File size**: 15–30% of the original.
- **Quality**: Visible if you look closely; unnoticeable in normal browsing.
- **Typical scenarios**: WeChat Moments, Weibo, chat sharing.

**60 is the sweet spot for most scenarios** — quality loss is nearly invisible and file size drops dramatically.

### 70–80: The Universal Recommendation

- **File size**: 30–50% of the original.
- **Quality**: The naked eye can barely tell it apart from the original.
- **Typical scenarios**: Sharing where good quality needs to be preserved while keeping size in check.

Level 70 fits professional sharing. Level 80 is right for pre-archiving important photos.

### 90: Near-Lossless

- **File size**: 60–80% of the original.
- **Quality**: Almost identical to the original.
- **Typical scenarios**: Archival material, pre-press preparation.

Going higher than 90 rarely helps. A JPG at 100 is larger than one at 90 with no perceivable quality gain.

## What the 1–99 Free Slider Is For

The nine presets cover about 90% of scenarios, but sometimes finer control is needed:

- **65**: A touch better than 60, a touch smaller than 70.
- **75**: A touch better than 70, a touch smaller than 80.
- **85**: A touch better than 80 but still under control.
- **50 / 55**: Find a better balance between 40 and 60.

Don't guess the level. **Use your eyes** to compare and pick the right one.

## Recommended Levels by Content Type

| Content type | Recommended level | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Pure-text screenshots | 30–50 | Text edges need to stay sharp, but backgrounds are simple. |
| Ordinary photos | 60–75 | Balance between quality and size. |
| Portrait close-ups | 75–85 | Skin texture cannot survive low-quality compression. |
| Landscape photos | 60–80 | Lots of color gradations; below 60 shows banding. |
| Logos / design files | Use PNG instead | Vector art and flat colors don't belong in JPG. |
| Screen-recording stills | 40–60 | Heavy text and icons; low quality blurs them. |
