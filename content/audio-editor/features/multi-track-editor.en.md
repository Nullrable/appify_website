## Multi-track audio editor

A recording, a song, a podcast - more often than not you're not starting from scratch, you're stitching together pieces that already exist and shaping them. The multi-track editor drops those pieces onto one shared timeline and lets you adjust each track on its own.

## How the timeline works

You can place multiple audio clips at once, each on its own track. Tracks stack vertically; the horizontal axis is time.

- **Trim** - A clip has a handle on each end. Drag the start, drag the end, throw away what you don't want.
- **Split** - Cut one clip into two at any time point and edit each part separately.
- **Rearrange** - Drag clips within a track or across tracks to reorder them.
- **Speed** - Continuous slider from 0.25x to 4x. Slow down or speed up.
- **Reverse** - One tap to flip playback. Handy for transition sound effects.

## Enhancement effects

Each clip can have its own stack of effects, in any order:

- **Channel split / merge** - Pull stereo apart into mono, or merge mono into stereo
- **Noise reduction** - Tame floor and ambient noise
- **Voice enhance** - Bring out vocal frequencies, soften the backing
- **Normalize** - Align loudness across clips
- **Stereo widening** - Open up the soundstage

Effects stack, and the order changes the result - drag them around and listen to find what works.

## Drafts save automatically

The parameters from trimming, speed, reverse, and enhancement are written into the local draft as you drag. Switch screens and come back - your settings are still there. Drafts live entirely on your iPhone; uninstalling the app deletes them all.

## The source file doesn't move

Everything that happens on the timeline happens inside the app's sandbox directory. **The original audio is never touched** - keep the source, use the new version, whatever you want.

## Export

Before exporting you can preview the whole thing inside the app. After export, the new file lands in the app's sandbox with a new name and a prompt asks you to "Save to Files" to place it. Output formats: MP3, WAV, FLAC, or OGG.
