## Why 30 seconds

A ringtone that drags on gets old fast, and a long one makes it hard to find "the right line." Under 30 seconds is a sweet spot for iPhone ringtones: enough room for a full chorus, a turn-of-phrase, or a clean hook.

This guide uses that window and walks you through the full flow from picking素材 to syncing it onto your iPhone.

## Step 1: pick your source

Choose audio you'd be happy hearing for six months at a time when the phone rings:

- A song's hook or chorus
- A memorable line from a podcast
- The first time your kid said "mama" or "papa"
- Your own catchphrase
- A piece of game音效 or a classic movie line

Drop the素材 into your iPhone's Photos or the Files app; the audio editor reads from there.

## Step 2: trim to 30 seconds

Open the audio editor, import the素材, and drag the two handles on the timeline:

- **In-point** - skip the intro, dive straight into the line you want to hear
- **Out-point** - stay under 30 seconds. iOS allows 40, but shorter feels tighter
- **Preview** - listen through once in-app to confirm the cut feels right

The audio is trimmed inside the app sandbox; the original file is never touched.

## Step 3: make it sit nicely in the ear

A clean source trimmed down often works as-is, but if the source is too quiet or has hard edges, you can polish further:

- **Fade in** - rises from silence over a few seconds, suits gentler素材
- **Fade out** - ends naturally instead of popping off
- **Loudness normalization** - if the source is too quiet, the ringtone might be missed; pull it up to a consistent level

None of these are required. Most of the time a clean hook cut to length is enough.

## Step 4: export M4R

Once you're happy, export as M4R. M4R is the iOS-only ringtone container - essentially AAC audio with a ringtone flag. Small file size, broad compatibility.

## Step 5: sync to your iPhone

- **Mac users** - drag the .m4r file into the "Tones" tab for your iPhone in Finder
- **Windows users** - drag the .m4r file into iTunes' "Tones" library

Once synced, the new ringtone appears under "**Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Ringtone**" on your iPhone.

## A few tips

- **Drop into the chorus** - skip the intro; the second line of the chorus usually has the strongest impact
- **Don't start at 0 seconds** - iOS ringtones fade in from silence, so starting at the素材's true 0 sounds flat
- **3-5 seconds of fade out** - give it a clean tail, don't let a pop ruin the whole feel
- **Confirm with headphones** - speakers and headphones sound very different; listen with headphones in-app before exporting

## Fully local

Trimming, fades, normalization, M4R encoding - all run on the iPhone. Source isn't uploaded, intermediate files don't sit on a server, and the process works without a network.
