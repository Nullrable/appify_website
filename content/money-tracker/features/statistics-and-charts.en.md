The Statistics tab is where a tracker stops being a list and starts telling you something.

## Three views of the same data

A category pie chart sits next to two trend lines: one over the last 30 days, one over the last 12 months. Filter by expense, income, or profit, and filter by account range to answer questions like "how much did I spend on cash this quarter?" The charts, the drill-downs and the per-account lists all read from the same local data, so they never disagree with each other.

## Tap any slice

Select a slice of the pie or a row of the trend and the App opens the transactions behind it: paginated, searchable by note keyword, and swipe-to-delete. You do not have to guess which entries produced a number - the number leads you to the entries.

## One list per account

Every account has a list view and a month view, with a year view layered on top for long histories. The month grid and the day grid follow the first-day-of-week setting you choose, so the calendar reads the way you think.

## Numbers you can trace

Every figure in the statistics comes from the transactions you recorded. No estimation, no smoothing, no anonymous aggregation - which is also why a forgotten transaction shows up as a missing bar. The charts and the ledger are the same data seen from two sides.
