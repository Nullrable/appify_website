## Image Compression

Some photos you want to keep — but not at their original size. A 6 MB panorama of last summer's hike, a high-res scan of an old document, a portrait you like but rarely reopen. Image Compression shrinks these files so they take up a fraction of the space while remaining visually identical.

### Pick the size, see the savings

Open an image, choose a target — smaller file, balanced, or near-original quality — and the compression screen shows the current size and the projected size side by side. You see the trade-off before you commit, and the decision is yours.

### Resize and recompress in one pass

The screen lets you cap the longest edge at a chosen resolution. Photos already at or below that resolution are recompressed only; photos above it are resized and then recompressed. Either way, you end up with a single new version that is dramatically lighter.

### Save where you choose

Compressed images can be saved back to the same album or to a new one, depending on whether you want to replace the original or keep both. The original is only replaced after you confirm.

### Runs entirely on your device

Compression happens locally using on-device image processing. The photo is never uploaded for compression, and the new version is written directly to your library. No account, no server round-trip, no quality loss from re-encoding over a network.
