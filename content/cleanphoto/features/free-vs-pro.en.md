## Free and Pro

CleanPhoto's core is free: every scanner, every view, no limits on how much you scan or review. The difference between Free and Pro comes down to one moment — the delete confirmation.

### What is always free

All three detectors — exact duplicates, similar and burst photos, screenshots — plus the month-by-month Swipe view and all review tools are available to everyone. There is no scan quota, no group cap and no locked category.

### Deleting in the free version

When you confirm a deletion in the free version, a short ad plays first, and the deletion completes after it. That is the entire trade: a few seconds of ad per deletion session, instead of a subscription.

### What Pro changes

Pro removes that step. Deletions run immediately with no ad, which matters most in big cleanup sessions where you confirm group after group. Pro is a subscription with weekly and annual plans, billed through your app store account, and you can cancel anytime from your store subscription settings.

### Which one fits you

If you clean occasionally — a screenshot sweep every few months, a duplicate pass after a holiday — Free does everything you need; the occasional ad costs a few seconds. If you maintain a large library, clean in monthly sessions, or simply want the fastest possible workflow, Pro removes the only remaining point of friction.

### Either way, the same rules apply

Both versions scan entirely on your device, never upload your photos, and never delete anything without your explicit confirmation.
