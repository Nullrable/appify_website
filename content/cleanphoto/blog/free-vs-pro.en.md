## CleanPhoto Free vs Pro: Which One Fits the Way You Clean?

Subscription fatigue is real, so here is the honest version of the comparison. CleanPhoto's Free and Pro differ in exactly one moment of the workflow — everything else is the same product. This post lays out what that means in practice, so you can pick the version that matches how often you clean.

### What you get either way

All three detectors — exact duplicates, similar and burst photos, screenshots — plus the month-by-month Swipe view and every review tool are available to everyone. There is no scan quota, no group cap, no locked category. If a feature exists in the app, you can use it without paying.

### The one difference

In the free version, when you confirm a deletion, a short ad plays first and the deletion completes right after it. That is the entire trade: a few seconds of ad per deletion session instead of a subscription. Pro removes that step — deletions run immediately, with no ad. In a big cleanup session where you confirm group after group, that saved wait adds up.

### What Pro is, concretely

Pro is a subscription with weekly and annual plans, billed through your app store account, and you can cancel anytime from your store's subscription settings. There is no separate account to create and nothing to configure — it is a standard store subscription.

### Choosing by habit, not by feature list

If you clean occasionally — a screenshot sweep every few months, a duplicate pass after a holiday — Free does everything you need, and the occasional ad costs a few seconds. If you maintain a large library, clean in monthly sessions, or simply want the fastest possible workflow, Pro removes the only remaining point of friction. The honest summary: Light users lose nothing by staying free. Heavy users gain time.

### The rules that never change

Both versions scan entirely on your device, never upload your photos, require no account for scanning, and never delete anything without your explicit confirmation. Upgrading changes the ad, not the privacy model — the Pro version works exactly the same on-device way, it just skips the ad step.

### A practical suggestion

Not sure which you are? Start free. Use it through one real cleanup — a screenshot backlog, a burst-photo sweep — and notice whether the ad wait actually bothers you. If it does, Pro is one tap away in the store; if it does not, you have a fully working cleaner that cost nothing.
